Takedown Requests

Wingato hosts no content — everything is streamed from the source library. If you believe a file is listed wrongly, the fastest route is the source library itself:

  • Internet Archive: report via the item's page on archive.org
  • Wikimedia Commons: report via the file's page on commons.wikimedia.org

You can also email us and we will pass it on:

takedown: takedown@wingato.example
