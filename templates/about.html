> ABOUT WINGATO

What is Wingato?

Wingato is a free downloader for openly-licensed and public-domain media. It searches the Internet Archive and Wikimedia Commons and lets you download films, audio, images and text that are free to use.

Why only open sources?

Downloading videos from YouTube, Instagram, Facebook or X breaks those platforms' terms of service, and hosting a tool that does it gets the site taken down. By sticking to libraries that want their media downloaded, Wingato can stay online and stay legal.

How does it work?

Type what you're looking for. Wingato queries the source libraries' public APIs, shows you what's available, and streams the file straight to you. Nothing is stored on our servers.

Licensing note

Every result links back to its source page, where you can see the exact licence (public domain, CC0, CC-BY, etc.). Some CC licences require attribution — check the source page before you reuse something.

Wingato does not host any files. We do not control what is in the source libraries; we only provide access to what they publish.
